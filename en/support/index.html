Support

Got a question, found a bug or have an idea? Drop me a line.

Email: my-inner-core-app@proton.me

I usually reply within a few days. Please don’t send me anything from the app you’d rather keep private. It helps if you include the app version and your iPhone model.

You’ll also find many answers right in the app, under Settings › Help.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. The app stores your pillars, goals, steps, sparks, your inbox and your weekly check-ins locally only. There’s no account and no server, and I have no access to your content.

After a weekly check-in, the app also saves a copy to your own iCloud Drive (at most one a day; the five newest are kept). You’ll find them in the Files app under iCloud Drive › My Inner Core. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

The safe way is a backup file:

  1. On your old iPhone, go to Settings › “Export data”. Save the file to iCloud Drive, for example, or AirDrop it to yourself.
  2. On your new iPhone, install My Inner Core, then go to Settings › “Import data” and pick the file. Importing replaces all data on that iPhone.

If both iPhones are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account, you can skip the export: when importing, pick the newest automatic copy from the My Inner Core folder in iCloud Drive. On first launch, the app also offers “Restore from backup” for this.

An iPhone backup or Quick Start should bring your data across too, but that hasn’t been confirmed yet, so the backup file is the way to go for now.

How do I turn off the lock?

In the app, go to Settings › Privacy and turn off “Lock app”. The app will then open without Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

How do I turn the usage statistics on or off?

The app asks you once, when you first set it up, whether you’d like to share them. You can change your mind any time in the app under Settings › Privacy › “Share usage statistics”. While the switch is off, the app sends no events. The statistics never include your content, only things like “check-in completed”. The privacy policy lists exactly what’s sent.

How do I delete all my data?
  1. In the app, go to Settings › “Delete all data”. This removes all content from that iPhone.
  2. If you no longer need the app, delete it from your iPhone.
  3. Delete the automatic copies: Files app › iCloud Drive › My Inner Core.
  4. Also delete any export files you’ve saved yourself.

I don’t hold any of your content, so there’s nothing on my side to delete.

Which iOS version do I need?

My Inner Core is an iPhone app and needs iOS 27 or later.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. My Inner Core is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Is My Inner Core a form of therapy?

No. It’s a tool for self-reflection and goal setting, and it doesn’t replace therapy, counseling or treatment. If you’re going through a hard time, please reach out to people you trust or to a professional.
