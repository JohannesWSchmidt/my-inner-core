My Inner Core

My Inner Core helps you grow on purpose. You choose three to six pillars that matter most in your life and give each one goals, steps and small sparks. Thoughts that come to you during the day go straight into your inbox. In the weekly check-in you look at each pillar, rate how satisfied you are and pick your weekly focus, so over time you can see where you’re heading. It’s a tool for self-reflection and goal setting, not therapy or treatment. Your content stays on your iPhone, with no account and no server. The app is free and has no ads.
